## Assessment

MoO can be presented coherently as an ongoing philosophical and mathematical
inquiry with an inspectable computational model. Its central account is the
dependence of construction on the immediate `1`: remembered recurrence,
relations between the resulting multiples, and speculation on convergences
across those orders. With each iteration of `1`, the full set of fundamental
operations unfolds across the available pairs. This account should lead the
presentation; the research programs then show what it motivates and what has
actually been checked.

The hierarchy's philosophical motivation is present. Treating it as an
unexplained ranking, or reducing it to an arbitrary software permission, was
an error in the initial review. The editorial correction makes the dependency
argument explicit and connects it to the examples.

The project has enough substance to share as work in progress. This review
does not establish a novelty claim or certify a completed mathematical
foundation. The value a reader can assess is the account itself, its explicit
constructions, and the research questions it supports.

## Remaining Work And Decisions

The full signed operator-fan field is broader than the implemented
positive-spine corpus. Its confirmation and operand rules need mathematical
development before a generator can faithfully implement them. This review
has not changed the foundations or supplied those missing implementation rules.

Historical research has uneven reproducibility. Five named historical source
databases are absent; saved reports retain their identity but cannot substitute
for those inputs. Fresh examples now demonstrate the current tools, while
exact historical reproduction remains conditional on the required sources
and environments.

The review covered the canonical account, repository organization, research
program maps, selected proofs and certificates, executable examples, and the
full automated test suite. It did not independently re-prove every archived
result or rerun research whose required inputs are unavailable.

Publication remains the author's decision. No license was chosen and nothing
was published. Before a public release, choose which historical conversations
and research records belong in the public edition, and settle its license.
The philosophy and runnable introduction can stand together without waiting
for every open research program to reach a conclusion.
