# Supporting Research

> Status: active research router.

The [main account](../README.md) presents Modulus of One's philosophy through branch
inspection: repeated relations, their interactions, proposed boundaries and
further constructions. The programs here develop those investigations. They
supply supporting arguments, experiments, and open leads; each must be
assessed on its own evidence.

Research is organized by stable question and subject area. Directory placement
does not certify a claim. Each program card records its evidence layer, claim
status, tools, versioned results, negative findings, missing inputs, and next
admissible work.

Research notes do not change Modulus of One runtime semantics. A result gains weight only
when it returns to exact graph evidence, names its controls or holdouts, and is
read through the [canonical claim boundary](../docs/CLAIMS.md) and the
[research-program protocol](../docs/protocols/research-programs.md).

## Reading Rule

Read a program in this order:

1. Program card for current scope and disposition.
2. Detailed notes in the card's stated order.
3. Actual tool and test dependencies, which may differ from the conceptual
   progression.
4. Frozen preregistration, report, analysis, and corpus inventory.
5. Negative findings and open obligations before any next experiment.

A missing report, stopped design, or rejected subclaim must be visible in the
card. It is not a reason to erase the surrounding program. Historical material
moves to the archive only after a named active owner or explicit disposition has
been established.
