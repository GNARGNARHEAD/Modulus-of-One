# Framing Lenses Note

> Status: peripheral research framing.
>
> This note keeps outside mathematical research in its proper place. Gauss,
> Euler, Fourier, Hodge, Ramanujan, Hardy, Stern-Brocot, Calkin-Wilf, continued
> fractions, integer complexity, experimental mathematics, and related bodies
> of work are not part of Modulus of One's core grammar. They are framings used to ask
> better questions after Modulus of One has recorded witnessed emergence.

## Boundary

Modulus of One's core is not imported from outside mathematics.

The core remains:

```text
1
fundamental operator fan
orders
branches
witnessed emergence
field of observation
projected form
```

External research does not define any of these terms.

External research also does not validate Modulus of One by association. A famous name,
classical theorem, or standard method is not Modulus of One evidence. The evidence is
still the witnessed emergence itself.

## Allowed Role

Outside research is allowed as a framing lens when it helps ask:

```text
What branch or relation should be inspected?
What ordinary explanation might account for this pattern?
What false reading should be guarded against?
What projection might reveal or obscure witnessed emergence?
What control or comparison would make the observation less fragile?
```

This means a framing lens may suggest a question, a comparison, or a possible
projection. It may not become the answer.

## Not Core

Do not put outside research into the root claim stack as if it is part of Modulus of One's
origin.

Bad reading:

```text
Euler explains Modulus of One.
Gauss's congruences define or validate Modulus of One.
Fourier/Hodge defines Modulus of One edge structure.
Stern-Brocot is the Modulus of One rational field.
Ramanujan-style projection licenses Modulus of One constants.
Hardy-Littlewood style arcs define Modulus of One circles or primes.
```

Better reading:

```text
Euler helps frame reciprocal-mass, product, zero, and pi-shadow questions.
Gauss helps frame quotient-domain, representation-witness, and unit-boundary questions.
Fourier/Hodge helps frame projection and edge-flow questions.
Stern-Brocot/Calkin-Wilf/Farey help frame rational-baseline questions.
Integer complexity helps frame witnessed-cost questions.
Ramanujan/Hardy-Littlewood help frame later asymptotic and projection questions.
Experimental mathematics helps frame discipline, controls, and kill rules.
```

## Current Use By Family

### Gauss

Gauss is useful here because his congruence theory makes the modulus-one
boundary exact: every integer collapses to one residue class, while his work on
quadratic forms keeps represented values distinct from their representation
witnesses. Article 39 also gives an explicit example of changing a unit-boundary
convention to preserve a global identity.

Modulus of One use:

```text
use congruence modulo 1 as a maximally coarse integer projection and negative
control; ask which construction witnesses survive value collapse
```

Modulus of One non-use:

```text
do not identify Ref(1) with Z/1Z, collapse rational nodes under an integer
theorem, or claim Gauss supplies Modulus of One's core or validates it
```

See [GAUSS_CONGRUENCE_AND_MODULUS_ONE_LENS_NOTE.md](number_theory/GAUSS_CONGRUENCE_AND_MODULUS_ONE_LENS_NOTE.md).

### Euler

Euler is useful because much of his work reads constants through exact
structures: sums, products, zeros, reciprocal powers, and factorization.

Modulus of One use:

```text
frame exact finite reciprocal-square, product, return, and pi-shadow questions
```

Modulus of One non-use:

```text
do not claim Modulus of One constructs pi because Euler relates pi to reciprocal squares
```

### Fourier / Hodge / Spectral Methods

These are useful because Modulus of One is not only a value list. It records witnessed
relations, and some relations can collapse under a value projection while
remaining meaningful as edge structure.

Modulus of One use:

```text
frame questions about projection, cancellation, return, preservation, and
edge-flow structure
```

Modulus of One non-use:

```text
do not replace witnessed emergence with spectral summaries
```

### Rational Enumeration Baselines

Stern-Brocot, Calkin-Wilf, Farey order, continued fractions, and related
rational systems are useful baselines for the positive-spine rational shadow.

Modulus of One use:

```text
ask whether a Modulus of One ordering, witness pattern, or branch visibility is explained
by ordinary rational height, denominator, tree depth, or continued-fraction
structure
```

Modulus of One non-use:

```text
do not identify Modulus of One itself with a rational enumeration tree
```

### Integer Complexity And Construction Cost

Integer complexity, addition chains, straight-line programs, and circuit-style
costs are useful because Modulus of One cares how a value emerges, not merely whether it
appears.

Modulus of One use:

```text
frame witnessed-cost comparisons across branches and fields of observation
```

Modulus of One non-use:

```text
do not reduce Modulus of One witness to a standard shortest-expression score
```

### Ramanujan / Hardy-Littlewood / Asymptotic Work

This material is useful only after exact Modulus of One structure exists. It can help
frame projected forms, asymptotic shadows, and later organization tests.

Modulus of One use:

```text
frame careful Order-4 projection questions from named source families
```

Modulus of One non-use:

```text
do not use asymptotic prestige to promote a weak pattern
```

### Experimental Mathematics

Experimental mathematics is useful as discipline: controls, holdouts,
counterexamples, exact reports, and kill rules.

Modulus of One use:

```text
protect the project from mistaking excitement for evidence
```

Modulus of One non-use:

```text
do not turn Modulus of One into target-hunting for impressive constants or famous
patterns
```

## Interpretation Rule

When a research note invokes an outside body of work, translate it back into
Modulus of One language before treating it as meaningful:

```text
outside framing
-> Modulus of One question
-> witnessed emergence or absent witness
-> branch / order / field reading
-> possible projected form only after repeated structure
```

If the chain cannot be completed, the outside research remains a framing note,
not a Modulus of One result.

## Governing Sentence

```text
External research frames questions around Modulus of One; it does not enter the core,
replace witnesses, or promote projected forms by authority.
```
