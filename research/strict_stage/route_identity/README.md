# Pascal And Route Identity

> Program status: completed controlled calibration with a rejected
> Pascal-specific signal and a narrower retained route-certificate result.

## Exact Question

Does a Pascal- or 216-labeled value exhibit construction structure beyond
ordinary strict-generator multiplicity, and which distinctions survive when
directed, commutative, and associative/commutative route identities are
explicitly separated?

## Relation To The Core

Route identity is graph-native only when it is expressed through exact retained
construction events or declared certificates. Pascal and geometric labels are
external frames and do not establish salience by themselves.

## Current Disposition

- The local Pascal recurrence signal is mostly a null under appropriate
  additive controls.
- Raw incoming route mass at `216` matches exact strict-generator expectations
  and is not salience evidence.
- Directed, commutative, and associative/commutative route quotients are
  meaningfully different and must be named.
- Declared cube, binomial-value, and side-cube certificates preserve different
  dependencies until the selected quotient collapses them.
- Semantic independence beyond the declared certificate system remains
  unresolved.

## Allowed And Forbidden Language

Allowed: route-identity discipline; quotient-specific certificate comparison;
controlled multiplicity null.

Forbidden: Pascal explains Modulus of One; `216` is exceptional because it has many
incoming events; two geometric labels necessarily represent independent graph
routes.
