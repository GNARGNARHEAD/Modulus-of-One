## Fixed scope

1. Read actual witnesses n+n, n*n and 3*n for n=1..128 in the saved current
   U160 corpus. Use n<=64 for calibration and 65..128 as a continuation check.
   Compare occurrence-stage coincidences, same-source equalities and shared
   output values as three different predicates. Do not rename the output value
   axis as runtime time. Expect stage=max(operands), 2/3-multiple output matches
   at 6k, and square/self-addition output matches at 4k^2 with different sources.
2. Include positive rational-square roots p/q, with coprime p,q<=H, at
   H=4,8,16 and continuation H=32,64. Compare S_H and c*S_H for
   c=1,4,9/4,2,3,1/2. In the unbounded rational domain an exact match requires
   c to be a rational square; otherwise none exists. Count all finite matches,
   and audit the first, middle and last ordered match at each H, or the
   fixed root pair (1,1) when no match exists. Keep raw representations scaled
   by 1 and 2 as distinct witnesses. Finite-window nonmatches for square c
   must not be described as a failure of the unbounded equivalence.
3. Inspect the primitive integer near-alignment n^2-2m^2=+/-1 for 1<=m<=512.
   Enumerate by exact square tests independently of a generator. Check the
   known classical recurrence (n,m)->(n+2m,n+m), starting at (1,1), with
   m<=32 calibration and 33..512 continuation. Record squares, doubling,
   directed differences, root transitions and operand readiness. Predict a
   period-two residue sign in recurrence index, not equally spaced graph
   stages. Compare raw scales 1,2 and common root denominators 1,3. Preserve
   the normalized signed error (n^2-2m^2)/m^2, not raw absolute proximity.
   For neighboring pairs (n+1,m), distinguish failure of the raw unit-gap test
   from the primitive test after gcd normalization; do not assume every
   normalized neighbor is a negative control.
4. Reinspect the already-seen area report as retrospective context only.
   Record first definite-classification counts for its three methods on the
   dyadic N schedule and their certificate checkpoints. This seven-point,
   deliberately scheduled record cannot establish a natural period or a
   pi-specific recurrence. No Fourier peak fitting or period extrapolation.

## Provisional inspection definition

An observed branch rhythm must name the field, repeated relations, event
predicate, clock, normalization and witness records. Exact value agreement,
same-stage occurrence and a recurring signed discrepancy are distinct events.
The branch is still the repeated relation; a clock is part of the inspection.

Primary metrics are agreement with exact event/stage predictions, missing or
inadmissible witnesses, rational match counts, and recurrence coverage in the
fixed envelope. Any unexplained mismatch weakens the proposed interpretation.
Ordinary arithmetic or stage rules explaining a pattern preclude calling it
a new MoO timing law. A clock-dependent pattern is reported with that clock.

The Pell recurrence is a classical calibration, not a novel theorem or a
result selected from pi data. The square-class argument and recurrence proof
will be retained alongside finite evidence. No periodicity is inferred merely
from repeating a word or plotting sparse data at chosen times.
