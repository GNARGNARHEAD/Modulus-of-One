# Research Coverage Of Node Profiles

Date: 2026-11-04

The node-profile layer makes selected mathematical associations and their
construction evidence searchable together. This audit accounts for all 14
entries in the [research map](README.md) and all 17 lenses in the
[transition ledger](../docs/protocols/research-programs.md). Coverage of a
research area does not mean that every result has become a node property.
The [profile note](../docs/software/NODE_PROFILES.md) documents the implemented queries;
the [claim boundary](../docs/CLAIMS.md) remains unchanged.
The subsequent [coherence review](PROFILE_COHERENCE_REVIEW_20261104.md)
records the root-binding and checkpoint-scope corrections and explains why
broad family overlap and recipe-driven stage differences need restrained interpretation.

## What Was Checked

All program cards were inspected for scope, status, sources and open obligations.
Targeted code and retained report checks then identified reusable definitions,
request bundles, participant roles, source identities and import hazards. These
checks included missing-file verification; a linked historical result was not
assumed reproducible merely because its JSON survives.

Finite live re-audits checked the closest shell certificates against the current
U576 source. The nine-request normalizations of `(3,4,5)` and `(6,8,10)` complete
at U25 and U100; `(8,15,17)` has factor support at U25 and direct square-sum
support at U225. The surviving U576/U80 and U160 source hashes were checked
against their retained calibration records. This is targeted revalidation,
not an exhaustive reproof, a rerun of every experiment, or a new general law.

## Closest Integration Decisions

One association may have several named certificates. Its exact arithmetic
membership does not depend on whether any one certificate is graph-supported.
Each certificate retains its requests, participant roles, dependencies, source
snapshot, selected checkpoint, blockers and observed completion stage. Earliest
support means earliest among these named certificates, not a minimum over every
possible proof. Existing literal criteria remain individually inspectable.

For a positive rational square root `p/q`, an integer-ratio certificate uses
`p*p`, `q*q`, `p/q`, and `p²/q²`. The identification of the quotient result
with `(p/q)²` is exact external algebra. It does not create a literal
fractional-input multiplication edge. Zero or signed cases require their own
explicit criteria rather than an automatic absolute-value substitution.

For a positive integer shell `(a,b,c)`, the full normalized certificate has
nine requests: three self-products, their square-sum agreement, two coordinate
divisions, and three divisions for the squared coordinates and normalized sum.
The existing [square-area inspection](geometry/square_area_inspection.py)
supplies this schema; its [report](geometry/results/2026-10-01-square-area-inspection-v1/report.json)
keeps square-relation and area evidence separate. Profiles reuse the former.
The `(3,4,6)` non-shell control gives normalized sum `25/36`, so coordinate
availability alone cannot qualify a tuple as a shell.

The normalized shell `(3/5,4/5,1)` has different certificates from `(3,4,5)`
and `(6,8,10)`, despite equal normalized values. Their U25/U100 completion
distinction is precisely the source information the profile must preserve.
Raw shell completion, coordinate normalization, normalized-square agreement,
and a later target-shell proof are different criteria and may occur at
different stages. The spiral's linked scalar transition does not require its
later direct target-shell certificate.

Comparison records shared directed arithmetic requests and source-scoped edge
occurrences. Repeated roles can refer to one edge; empty overlap is not proof
of independence. Source IDs from different snapshots are never comparable by
number alone. General associative/commutative expression merging is deferred:
the [route machinery](../moo/route_identity_probe.py) has explicit target
ancestry and leaf-cut rules, and its expression fingerprints omit premises.
Arbitrary profile bundles cannot safely inherit those fingerprints as identity.

Seven existing [Pascal-shell certificate definitions](strict_stage/route_identity/pascal_shell_calibration.py)
are now adapted into the selected `square:25` and `square:9` associations,
with every construction request and premise step retained. The
[retained calibration](strict_stage/route_identity/results/2026-09-22-pascal-shell-calibration-v1.json)
provides their comparison context. The shell target `625` has factored,
paired expansion, left expansion and direct composed-norm completion at U25,
U337, U544 and U576. The generic `81` controls complete at U9, U53 and U77.
These are named availability differences explained by operand readiness, not
a privileged Pascal timing mechanism. Optional AC-expression metadata is reused
from those definitions for construction steps only; premises are separately
audited and included in directed request comparisons. Equal expression metadata
never merges complete certificate identity or observed completion stages.
The reviewed adapter additionally binds each route to its association root
through the sum in the corresponding factored recipe. This adds `7+2 -> 9`
to the two generic expansions without altering the frozen source definitions
or their U53/U77 completion stages.

## Source Availability And Evidence Import

The [corpus inventory](experiments/CORPUS_INVENTORY.md) recorded an earlier
presence audit. On 2026-11-04, all five historical SQLite inputs were absent
at their named paths: `pascal_edge_probe_u300.sqlite`,
`pascal_cubic_scale_u1000.sqlite`, `circle_as_one_u40_wide.sqlite`,
`circle_as_one_u80.sqlite` under `out/experiments/`, and
`out/experiments/gauss/strict_stage_u850_holdout_v1.sqlite`. Older program-card
claims of local availability do not override this live check.

The surviving sources checked for this integration are the wide U576 and
narrow U80 files in `out/scratch/shell_composition_20260920_current_v1/`, and
`research/geometry/results/2026-10-01-square-area-inspection-v1/current_u160.sqlite`.
The association database is a derived snapshot, not an additional source corpus.

Historical JSON can still provide exact reported findings and experiment links.
It cannot supply current-corpus node/edge identities. Re-auditing a definition
against a surviving corpus is a new calibration; it does not restore a missing
historical input. Report and source hashes, bounds, checkpoint, evidence layer,
and observed versus predicted requests must survive any later import.

The [Euler evidence map](../docs/evidence/euler.md),
[geometry evidence map](../docs/evidence/geometry.md), and
[Gauss evidence index](experiments/gauss/README.md) retain the report routes.
Additional gaps remain explicit in their owners: the April Fermat input and
saved Fermat reports, R100/convergence summaries, and named motif-mass/aperture
outputs are absent. The closure input ledgers survive but use different operands
and clocks. No spectral tool, unified dynamics probe or prime-factor tool exists.

## Next Useful Connections

After this certificate integration, the smallest further shell extension is the
factor/quotient criteria already audited by the [shell-branch probe](../moo/shell_branch_search.py).
The `(8,15,17)` factor/direct U25/U225 contrast is a concrete test case.
Next, the [finite-series probe](../moo/series_probe.py) already exposes the
distinction between ingredients, quotient endpoints and linked recurrence:
`3/2` is both a geometric partial sum and an Euler-product endpoint, while its
saved rational accumulation lineage is blocked. These roles are useful without
claiming a new correlation law.

Quadratic-form fibres and exact phase relationships offer broader extensions
once their source and representation fields fit cleanly. External unit-distance
configurations, area refinements, spectral scouting and analytic experiments
need richer objects than scalar tags. Keep them discoverable through their
programs and evidence maps until that object model is justified by an inspection
question. The current work changes discoverability and certificate comparison;
it does not change construction, confirmation, operand eligibility or foundations.
