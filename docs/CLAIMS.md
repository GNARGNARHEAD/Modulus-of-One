## Claim Discipline

Every material claim states its `evidence_layer` and `claim_status` using the
[research protocol's canonical tokens](protocols/research-programs.md#canonical-status-values).
The graph is primary evidence for a Modulus of One claim. A value, approximation,
constant or shape label, or high score is insufficient without its nodes,
construction edges, repeated witnesses, input neighborhood, operation
signature, stage/confirmation status, and corpus bounds.

```text
A value appearing is not enough.
The same value can appear by different witnesses, and the witness matters.
The value is not the object; the witnessed emergence is the object.
```

Projected-form claims additionally identify their source family, projection
rule, primary metric, controls, holdout and kill rule. The complete certificate
and evidence standard are in the [Order-4 protocol](protocols/order4-projection.md).

## Foundational Commitments And Implementation Limits

These are the boundaries of the adopted model, not claims that its arithmetic
resources have been derived from immediate certainty:

- **Primitive certainty:** "I think, therefore `1`." Naming that certainty
  does not derive cardinality, a featureless whole or the absence of anything
  else. Memory, its content and classification, and descriptions of a present
  memory appearance remain uncertain. `Ref(1)` is the sole primitive runtime
  anchor; further construction depends on admitted operations, recurrence
  and relations of `1`.
- **Continuing operator fan:** `1+1 -> 2`, `1-1 -> 0`, `1*1 -> 1`, and
  `1/1 -> 1` begin the field. With each iteration, all four operations apply
  to all available ordered pairs and self-pairs. Positive counting is one
  branch, not the whole Modulus of One field. Zero and negative values are native
  cancellation/removal relations. Returning numerical `1` does not
  authenticate a witness or confer immediate certainty.
- **The attempted proof of `2`:** the proposed recurrence route requires
  preserving and reusing a prior `1`; it does not establish a second
  certainty. This is not an impossibility theorem about every conceivable
  derivation. "Infinite distance from `1`" describes the certainty gap,
  not a runtime metric.
- **Current operand rule:** at positive-spine stage `U_n`, only confirmed
  integers `1..n` are reusable operands. Retained outputs include unconfirmed
  integers, zero, negatives and rationals. They are real graph nodes to
  inspect, not errors or discarded values. A future positive integer becomes
  eligible only when the positive spine confirms it. Modulus of One speculates from
  confirmed iterations, not from speculations. The full signed-field
  confirmation rule remains unimplemented.
- **Orders of support:** recurrence through memory is second order;
  relations from confirmed recurrence, including fractions and multiplicative
  constructions, are third order. An earlier relational arrival and later
  counted confirmation can support the same node without rewriting the
  earlier witness. Exact arithmetic does not remove their dependency on
  admitted recurrence. See the [four orders](foundations/EPISTEMIC_ORDER.md#four-orders-of-support).
- **Value and construction identity:** one node represents each reduced
  rational value `p/q`; distinct local operation occurrences can reach that
  node. Value presence, a named witness and emergence through the branch
  being studied are different claims. Local edges do not select an ancestral
  supplying witness for each later operand use. The
  [runtime reference](software/RUNTIME.md) and [corpus contract](software/GRAPH_CORPUS.md)
  specify the representation.
- **Branches:** a branch is a repeated witnessed relation emerging along
  recurrence of `1`, not merely a formula family, value list, single edge or
  ordinary graph path. A value can participate in several branches. Its
  counted presence does not establish a desired branch witness that is
  permitted but not retained. Definitions and examples belong to the
  [branch vocabulary](foundations/BRANCHES.md) and
  [branch evidence protocol](protocols/branch-evidence.md).
- **Fourth-order proposals:** infinitesimals and transcendentals share
  epistemic standing as hypothetical convergence points or forms grounded
  in inspection. They are not mathematically identified or attained
  endpoints. A proposed form is a non-operational analysis-layer shadow
  inferred from a named exact graph family by an explicit projection rule.
  It is not a runtime node, operand, confirmed fact, theorem or completed
  object inside strict Modulus of One. It is more abstract than Order 3, not more
  certain. Credibility requires organizing or predicting held-out strict
  graph structure better than controls; approximation accuracy alone is
  insufficient.

A conditional theorem about a selected family remains valid under its stated
assumptions; it does not make a proposed form a confirmed Modulus of One object or confer
first-order certainty. The [pi example](examples/PI_FOURTH_ORDER_EXAMPLE.md)
distinguishes this mathematical argument from its fourth-order interpretation.

## Current Strong Claims

Modulus of One currently provides a graph-first construction framework from the primitive
certainty represented as `1`, under the admitted rules above. Its current
positive-spine corpus records integer and rational constructions while
restricting operand use to confirmed positive-spine iterations. Confirmation
is stage-indexed and weaker than immediate certainty.

Construction witnesses, multiplicity, neighborhoods and confirmation status
are primary evidence. External concepts serve as scrutiny lenses rather
than runtime foundations. Modulus of One may propose projected forms from stable graph
families within the stated fourth-order boundary.

## Corpus-Conditioned Observations

A strict corpus may support statements that a node is present, first appears
at a named stage, was initially speculative and later confirmed, has listed
incoming witnesses or an operation histogram, shares input neighborhoods,
or belongs to a corpus passing strict alignment checks.

Such statements depend on:

```text
max_stage
max_abs_p
max_abs_q
max_abs_value
retention policy
operand policy
tool version
```

Name the corpus and controls rather than quoting its observations as intrinsic
mathematical facts. The [corpus inventory](../research/experiments/CORPUS_INVENTORY.md)
owns source identities, known availability and regeneration limits. A saved
report does not establish that its historical input survives.

## Candidate Claims

These remain research candidates, not established theory:

| Candidate | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Persistent accumulation | Construction-gathering nodes or regions that persist across stages and controls. |
| Unit-quadratic shells | Exact rational square/addition agreements present in strict graph corpora. |
| Euler reciprocal mass | Exact finite reciprocal-square term and partial-sum structures when their nodes and construction edges are present. |
| Order-4 projected forms | Non-operational projected constants, shape-like or asymptotic forms inferred from exact graph evidence by explicit rules. |
| Non-rational projected constants | `pi` and `e` as transcendental anchors; `sqrt(2)` and `phi` as algebraic irrational anchors. All remain proposed forms, not completed runtime objects. |
| Operation-word recurrence | Repeated symbolic edge patterns under a named strict corpus rule. |
| Neighborhood divergence | Differences between numerical closeness and construction-neighborhood closeness under a named metric. |

These require preregistered metrics, controls, held-out data, saved reports and
interpretation notes before gaining credibility. The
[research map](../research/README.md) and program cards record the dispositions
of particular studies; this list is not a promotion of every associated result.

## Not Yet Earned

Modulus of One cannot currently claim that it:

- defines the Euclidean circle or constructs `pi`, `e`, `sqrt(2)`, `phi`, or
  any completed non-rational constant;
- is chaotic, or has established attractors, bifurcations, Lyapunov exponents
  or strange attractors;
- proves new number theory, replaces standard foundations or invalidates
  ordinary arithmetic;
- proves arithmetic `2` from the certainty event represented as `1` alone;
- proves or justifies a second epistemic state from arithmetic zero, identifies
  zero with an unmarked condition or absolute absence, or makes the absence
  of certainty itself a second certainty;
- is positive-only, or relegates zero and negative values to mere artifacts.

The marked/unmarked route is an open formalization question, not a candidate
result. `1-1 -> 0` witnesses cancellation under admitted operations. It does
not establish that an unmarked condition is admissible, that marked and
unmarked conditions are non-collapsing and mutually exclusive, or that they
are jointly exhaustive over a specified domain.

Memory as a proposed other, internal distinction within one, and a possible
negative footprint of otherness remain open on the same terms. No footprint
or second epistemic state has been established. The two-zero divergence
compares admitted accumulation and cancellation; uncertainty about memory
does not force either result. See [the otherness inquiry](foundations/OTHERNESS.md).

Older or speculative notes may use stronger research language. They do not
become current strict claims without an explicit, evidenced promotion through
the relevant protocol.
