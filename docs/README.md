## Where Definitions Live

The foundations note owns the operator fan, orders, confirmation and operand
eligibility. The claim ledger states the boundaries of current claims and
evidence layers. The [branch glossary](foundations/BRANCHES.md) defines branch
vocabulary; the [otherness inquiry](foundations/OTHERNESS.md) develops the open
foundational questions. The software references specify representation and
retention. Research notes apply these definitions without silently changing
them.

## Research Standards

The [research and analysis protocol](protocols/research-programs.md) owns
program cards, lifecycle, evidence handling and general inspection rules.
Specialized contracts cover [branch evidence](protocols/branch-evidence.md),
[graph invariants](protocols/graph-invariants.md), and
[fourth-order projection](protocols/order4-projection.md).
The [detailed lens reference](research/LENSES.md) retains the 17 lenses' metrics,
controls and stopping criteria; program cards own their current dispositions.

File-level evidence maps serve the [Euler/zeta](evidence/euler.md) and
[shell geometry](evidence/geometry.md) programs. They route to frozen evidence;
they are not additional versions of the definitions above.

For contributions, see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md). The
[public snapshot guide](PUBLIC_RELEASE.md) records the approved archive scope
and licensing. The [layout record](../archive/reorganizations/20261107/README.md)
explains the move from the former flat repository and locates old paths.
